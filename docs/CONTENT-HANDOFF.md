### Hero Concept

The hero should visually communicate the idea of a **rocket launch**.

The animation should represent the reality of business growth:

- A rocket launches upward.
- During the launch, some elements/proposals may fail.
- Some visual elements can represent:
  - Failed ideas
  - Wasted money
  - Poor campaigns
  - Unprofessional execution
  - Missed opportunities
  - Business losses
- These elements can burn, fall away, or disappear during launch.
- QAVYX should be visually represented as the system that helps the business continue moving upward.

### Creative Direction

The animation should communicate:

> Growth is not about avoiding every failure. It is about having the right system to keep moving forward.

The animation must remain premium and professional. Avoid making the failure visuals look comedic or destructive.

# 67. Animation Direction

The QAVYX website should feel like a **digital growth launchpad**, not a generic agency website.

## Hero Animation

Primary animation concept:

**Rocket Launch → Obstacles / Failures → Forward Motion → Growth**

Possible visual sequence:

1. Rocket is prepared on launchpad.
2. Marketing/business elements appear around it.
3. Some elements fail during launch.
4. Failed elements burn/fall away.
5. Rocket continues upward.
6. Growth indicators become stronger.
7. QAVYX branding remains as the central system.

## Animation Principles

- Premium
- Smooth
- Purposeful
- Fast enough to maintain attention
- Avoid excessive animation
- Avoid distracting the user from CTA
- Support reduced-motion preferences
